HOW TO PLAY

▶ GOAL Run right and reach the glowing exit portal in each of the 50 levels. ▲ GRAVITY FLIPS Every few seconds gravity reverses. Floors become ceilings and ceilings become paths. Watch the bar at the top — when it turns red, a flip is coming! ☠ HAZARDS Avoid spikes. Jump over them — you can even jump while upside-down. Don't fall through gaps in the floor (or ceiling!). ☠ DEADLY MACHINES Laser gates blink on the 3-second rhythm — dash through when dark. Fire jets erupt from the floor and ceiling (they glow before firing). Falling rocks shake, then drop — don't stand under them. Big spikes need a full-height jump. Every level adds more of them, faster! ♟ PC CONTROLS A D or ◀ ▶ to run • SPACE W ▲ to jump • P pause • M mute ☎ TOUCH CONTROLS Use the on-screen arrows to run and the big jump button. Hold jump longer to jump higher. ★ SKINS Unlock a new skin every 5 levels. Finish all 50 to unlock the 2 legendary skins!
